05 · Usage Rules
◯

Logo Clearance

Keep 25% of the logo's diameter as clear space around it. Never crop the circle or distort the aspect ratio.

■

Color Over Photos

Always use the dark overlay gradient before placing text. Never put white text directly on a bright or busy image area.

Aa

Font Mixing

Oswald for Latin display text. Noto Sans Arabic for Darja. Inter for small labels and data. Never substitute with system fonts.

↔

Bilingual Layout

Arabic flows right-to-left and sits above or beside the Latin equivalent. Give each its own line; never interleave mid-word.

▲

Trip Posters

Destination name in Oswald uppercase, centered top third. Dates and price in Inter below. Terrain photo fills the background.

☀

Color by Terrain

Desert Gold for Sahara trips. Cedar Green for mountain/forest. Coast Blue for coastal. Terra Red for campfire and canyon content.

06 · Voice & Tone
Do

Speak in Algerian Darja, casual and motivational

"El move لي تفرح القلب 🍀"

"إحتياجاتك كل نهاية أسبوع..."

Don't

Use formal Modern Standard Arabic or corporate phrasing

Avoid: "نقدم لكم خدمات السياحة والمغامرة"

Do

Frame nature as emotional therapy and self-discovery

"خــايف تروح وتضــيع فيها"

Don't

Use tourism-industry language or itinerary-style descriptions

Avoid: "Day 1: Arrival and check-in..."
